Intervity Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Intervity ("the app", "we") is an open-source interview-preparation app for Android. This policy explains what data the app uses, where that data is stored, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

  • The app does not require an account.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your learning data stays on your device. We do not receive it.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data in a local database on your device only:

  • Learning progress, answers, mistakes, XP and session history.
  • Topics you choose or type.
  • Resume text, if you enter it. The app reads it on your device to personalise questions.
  • Voice profile and practice results, if you use voice features.

We do not have access to this data. It is not uploaded to our servers.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice features, the app asks for microphone permission. You can refuse or remove this permission at any time in Android Settings. The rest of the app continues to work.

On Android, speech-to-text uses your device's built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google's speech service). That service can process your audio under its own privacy policy. The app does not store your audio recordings or send them to us.

4. Downloads

The app can download model and content files from Hugging Face and from our content delivery network (BunnyCDN). To deliver these files, these services receive standard technical request data, such as your IP address. The app does not send your personal data with these requests. All downloads use encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: to download model and content files.
  • Microphone: for optional voice practice.
  • Audio settings: to manage echo cancellation during voice practice.
  • Wake lock: to keep the screen and audio active during a practice session.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with any third party.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. To delete all app data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Because we do not hold copies of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Security

Your data is protected by the security of your device and Android's app sandbox, which keeps other apps from reading it. Network downloads use HTTPS.

9. Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for job interviews. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at xenticaiworkforce@gmail.com.
